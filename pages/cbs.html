Character Building Society

The Character Building Society (CBS), Government College of Technology Bhakkar is devoted to shaping students into confident, responsible, and well-rounded individuals by providing a positive and encouraging platform for their growth. Through engaging activities like Qirrat, Naat, speech, and Bait Bazi competitions, CBS not only nurtures students' talents but also strengthens their moral values, confidence, and ability to express themselves.

We strive to maintain an atmosphere built on merit, fairness, and respect, where every student feels included and motivated to participate. By promoting creativity, teamwork, and leadership, CBS aims to help students develop strong character and the confidence they need to move forward in life and make a meaningful contribution to society.

Our goal is simple, "to guide students toward becoming better individuals who can make a meaningful difference in society."
