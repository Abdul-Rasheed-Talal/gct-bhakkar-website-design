What is CBS?

The Character Building Society (CBS) is a vital wing of GCT Bhakkar dedicated to the holistic development of students beyond academics and technical skills.

CBS focuses on instilling moral values, discipline, leadership qualities, and a sense of social responsibility in students. Through various activities, seminars, workshops, and community service programs, CBS ensures that our graduates are not only technically proficient but also ethically grounded individuals.

The society organizes regular events including motivational talks, character development workshops, cleanliness drives, tree plantation campaigns, and awareness programs that contribute to the personal growth of every student at GCT Bhakkar.
