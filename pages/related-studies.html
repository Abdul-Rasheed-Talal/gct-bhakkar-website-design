About Related Studies

The Related Studies Department is a foundational pillar at GCT Bhakkar, providing essential academic support across all technology programs.

This department covers the general and science subjects that form the backbone of every DAE program — including Applied Mathematics, Applied Physics, Applied Chemistry, English, Islamiat & Pak Studies, and Management subjects.

Our dedicated faculty ensures that students develop strong analytical thinking, communication skills, and scientific foundations that complement their technical training and prepare them for both industry and higher education.
